About Us

Discover our mission, values, and the people who make Example School a world-class institution.

Our Mission

At Example School, we are committed to providing a transformative educational experience that prepares students for success in an increasingly complex and interconnected world.

Through rigorous academics, character development, and a nurturing community, we empower students to become critical thinkers, compassionate leaders, and lifelong learners.

Our History

Founded in 1950, Example School has a rich tradition of academic excellence and innovation in education. For over seven decades, we have been shaping the minds and hearts of students from around the world.

From our humble beginnings with just 50 students, we have grown into a vibrant community of over 500 students and 50 faculty members, representing more than 30 countries.

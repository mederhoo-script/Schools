Application Process

We welcome applications from families who share our commitment to academic excellence and community values.

1

Submit Application

Complete our online application form and submit required documents including transcripts and recommendations.

2

Campus Visit

Schedule a campus tour to experience our school community and meet with faculty and students.

3

Interview & Assessment

Participate in a student interview and complete any required assessments.

4

Admission Decision

Receive your admission decision and enrollment information within 2-3 weeks.
